# 🚀 Easy Peasy Guide: How to Put KARA-OKEY on the Internet!

> **Target Audience:** Anyone, even a primary school student! No tricky programming words or scary terminal commands needed. 🎈

---

## 🌟 What Are We Doing? (The Simple Story)

Think of it like this:
1. **Your Computer:** Right now, KARA-OKEY is only inside your own laptop. Only people in your house can see it.
2. **GitHub (The Cloud Locker 🎒):** A safe online box on the internet where you store a copy of your project files.
3. **Vercel (The Magic Robot Chef 🤖):** An online service that grabs your files from GitHub, cooks them up into a real live website, and gives you a link (like `kara-okey.vercel.app`) that anyone in the world can open on their phone or tablet!

Best part? **It is 100% FREE!** 🎉

## Step 1: Create a Free GitHub Account 🐙

If you don't have a GitHub account yet:

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, etc.).
2. Go to **[github.com](https://github.com)**.
3. Click the big green button that says **Sign up**.
4. Type in:
   - Your email address
   - A password you will remember
   - A cool username (for example: `music-star-2026`)
5. Solve the little picture puzzle (to prove you are a human and not a robot 🤖).
6. Check your email for a verification code number, type it in, and you are in!

---

## Step 2: Install "GitHub Desktop" (The Easy Clicky App) 💻

Instead of typing complicated computer codes in a black terminal window, we will use a friendly app with buttons!

1. Go to **[desktop.github.com](https://desktop.github.com)**.
2. Click the big purple button: **Download for Windows**.
3. Once downloaded, open the installer (`GitHubDesktopSetup.exe`).
4. When it opens, it will ask you to **Sign in to GitHub.com**.
5. Click **Sign in**, your browser will pop up, click **Authorize desktop**, and you are connected!

---

## Step 3: Send KARA-OKEY to GitHub 📦

Now let's tell GitHub Desktop about your KARA-OKEY folder on drive `D:\`.

1. Open **GitHub Desktop**.
2. Click on **File** in the top menu bar, then click **Add local repository...** (or press `Ctrl + O`).
3. Click the **Choose...** button.
4. Browse to your project folder: `D:\KARA-OKEY`, and click **Select Folder**.
5. If GitHub Desktop says: *"This directory does not appear to be a Git repository"*, don't worry! Click the blue link that says:
   > **create a repository**
6. A little window pops up:
   - **Name:** `kara-okey`
   - **Local path:** `D:\` (or `D:\KARA-OKEY`)
   - Click **Create Repository**.
7. Now look at the top bar of GitHub Desktop. You will see a blue button that says:
   > **Publish repository**
8. Click **Publish repository**:
   - Make sure **Name** is `kara-okey`.
   - *Optional:* You can uncheck "Keep this code private" if you want friends to see the code, or leave it checked. Either way works with Vercel!
   - Click the blue **Publish repository** button.
9. Wait 10-20 seconds. Once the progress bar finishes, your project is officially saved in GitHub cloud! 🚀

---

## Step 4: Create a Free Vercel Account ⚡

Now we need the magic robot chef (Vercel) to turn that GitHub code into a real website.

1. Go to **[vercel.com/signup](https://vercel.com/signup)**.
2. Choose **"Hobby"** (the free personal plan).
3. Type in your name and click **Continue**.
4. Click the button: **Continue with GitHub**.
5. A GitHub authorization screen will appear asking: *"Authorize Vercel?"*
   - Click the green button: **Authorize Vercel**.
6. That's it! You are logged into your Vercel dashboard.

---

## Step 5: Press "Deploy" & Get Your Live Website Link! 🎊

This is the most exciting step!

1. On your Vercel home dashboard, look for the black button that says **"Add New..."** in the top right, and click **Project**.
2. You will see a list called **"Import Git Repository"**.
3. Look for **`kara-okey`** in the list.
   - *(If you don't see it, click the search box and type `kara-okey`, or click "Adjust GitHub App Permissions" and select your repository).*
4. Next to `kara-okey`, click the blue button: **Import**.
5. You will see a configuration screen:
   - **Project Name:** `kara-okey` (leave it as is)
   - **Framework Preset:** Next.js (Vercel automatically detects this! ✨)
   - **Root Directory:** `./` (leave it as is)
6. Click the big blue button at the bottom:
   > **🚀 Deploy**
7. **Watch the Magic!**
   - You will see little building animations and status lines.
   - In about 1 to 2 minutes, the screen will shower with **digital confetti**! 🎉
   - It will say: **"Congratulations! You just deployed a new Project to Vercel."**
8. Click on the big preview picture or click the link ending with `.vercel.app`.
9. **BAM! Your KARA-OKEY is live on the internet!** 🎤🎶
   - You can copy this link and send it to your phone, your friends, or your family!

## 🔄 Bonus: How to Update Your Website Later

What happens if you change colors, fix a song, or add new features in your project?

You **never** have to set up Vercel again! All you do is:

1. Edit and save your files in your code editor as usual.
2. Open **GitHub Desktop**.
3. You will see the changed files listed on the left side.
4. In the bottom-left corner:
   - Type a short message in the "Summary" box (e.g. `Fixed button color` or `New song added`).
   - Click the blue button: **Commit to main**.
5. At the top right, click **Push origin**.
6. **Vercel will notice immediately**, rebuild your site in 60 seconds, and your live website updates all by itself! 🪄

**No!** Both GitHub and Vercel are 100% free for personal hobby projects.

#### 2. "Does my computer need to stay turned on?"
**Nope!** Once deployed, Vercel hosts it in the cloud 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, even when your computer is shut down and you are asleep.

**Yes!** Anyone who opens your `your-project-name.vercel.app` link on an iPhone, Android, iPad, or computer can play karaoke!
